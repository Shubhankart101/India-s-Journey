A story in motion

The idea behind the atlas

Big change is easier to see, one signal at a time.

India’s story isn’t a single headline or a single number. This independent, public-data project brings together long-run indicators and current snapshots so you can explore what changed, when it changed, and how the evidence is measured.

Start with a theme, search for an indicator, and use the period controls to narrow the view. Hover for exact values, zoom into a moment, or open Deep Dive for the full series, notes and cited source. Every chart keeps its frequency and limitations visible; unavailable data is never filled with an estimate.

Made in appreciation of public-data storytelling by PolityPolicy, Polity and Policy and Indian Matrix. This is an independent project and does not represent those publishers.
