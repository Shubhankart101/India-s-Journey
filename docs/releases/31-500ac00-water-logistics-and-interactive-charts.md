# Water, Logistics, expanded air and rail data, interactive charts

- **Commit:** [`500ac00`](https://github.com/Shubhankart101/India-s-Journey/commit/500ac00c007c635b938707d9d4c7ce70b10f551d)
- **Date:** 2026-11-04

## What it delivered

- **Water Transport & Shipping** group (43 charts, six subgroups): port cargo, inland waterways, coastal shipping, passenger traffic, container performance and a global maritime comparison. Details: [WATER-TRANSPORT.md](../WATER-TRANSPORT.md).
- **Logistics & Freight** group (43 charts, six subgroups): road, rail, sea and air freight, modal split and logistics revenue. Rail, sea and air freight are repeated on purpose; the modal groups keep their own copies. Details: [LOGISTICS-AND-FREIGHT.md](../LOGISTICS-AND-FREIGHT.md).
- **Air Travel** grew from 27 to 108 charts using AAI Annual Reports 2019-20 to 2024-25, AAI Traffic News and parliamentary replies. **Railways** grew from 38 to 66 charts, and the World Bank freight and passenger trend lines now build.
- **Interactive charts:** toolbar with full-screen, CSV and PNG export, chart-type switch, log scale, range presets, legend isolation, pinned detail panel, group quick-jump and title search; choices persist in local storage.
- **Art:** 18 new India-specific scenes.
- **Tests:** per-layer suites for air, rail, water and logistics plus dashboard interactivity checks (235 tests in total).
