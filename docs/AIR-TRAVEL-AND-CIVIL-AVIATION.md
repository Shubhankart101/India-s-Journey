# Air Travel & Civil Aviation Data

## Overview

The **Air Travel & Civil Aviation** subgroup tracks India's aviation sector growth, connectivity expansion, and position in global air transport markets. It brings together official World Bank indicators, Ministry of Civil Aviation data, and international aviation statistics to show India's transformation from a limited aviation market to one of the world's fastest-growing markets.

| Subgroup | Series | Topics |
|---|---|---|
| Air Travel & Civil Aviation | 12 | Passenger traffic, cargo, airport infrastructure, fleet growth, connectivity |

## Key Indicators

### 1. Passenger Traffic & Market Growth

| Indicator | Source | Unit | Frequency | What It Shows |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Civil Aviation Passengers | World Bank (IS.AIR.PSGR) | Million passengers | Annual | Total passengers (domestic + international) carried on civil aviation flights |
| Aircraft Departures | World Bank (IS.AIR.DPRT) | Thousand departures | Annual | Total number of scheduled and non-scheduled flights |
| Passengers per 1,000 Population | Derived | Per 1,000 people | Annual | Aviation penetration rate; comparison with other economies |
| Year-on-Year Growth Rate | Derived | % change | Annual | Market expansion velocity; sensitivity to economic cycles |

**Interpretation:**
- India's aviation market has grown from ~50 million annual passengers (2010) to 140+ million (2023), a 3x expansion
- Growth rate fluctuations reveal economic cycles, policy reforms (Open Skies agreements), and external shocks (COVID-19, fuel prices)
- Passengers-per-capita is rising but remains below China, USA, Japan and Australia, showing headroom for continued growth

**Official Sources:**
- [World Bank Aviation Indicators](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/IS.AIR.PSGR?locations=IN)
- Ministry of Civil Aviation, Government of India - [Aviation Statistics](https://www.civilaviation.gov.in/)

## Policy & Regulatory Framework

### Government Initiatives

1. **Regional Connectivity Scheme (RCS)** — Subsidises routes to underserved tier-2 and tier-3 cities; ~500+ routes activated since 2016
2. **Air India Privatisation** — Strategic disinvestment improving operational efficiency
3. **Open Skies Agreements** — Bilateral and plurilateral air service agreements with 70+ countries
4. **Airport Modernisation** — New terminals, runway capacity at major hubs (Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore)
5. **Aircraft Leasing & MRO Policy** — To support fleet growth and maintenance ecosystem

### Regulatory Bodies
- Ministry of Civil Aviation (Policy)
- Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA) — Safety and airworthiness
- Airports Authority of India (AAI) — Government airport operations
- Bureau of Civil Aviation Security (BCAS) — Airport security

## Data Limitations & Caveats

1. **Passenger Counting:** "Passenger" = one person boarding one flight; a round trip counts as two passengers
2. **Air Cargo Units:** Measured in tonne-kilometres to account for distance; not raw tonnage
3. **Missing Recent Years:** World Bank indicators are 1-2 years behind publication; latest data comes from Ministry releases
4. **International vs Domestic Mix:** World Bank totals combine both; state-level domestic splits require separate MoCA reports
5. **COVID-19 Impact:** 2020-2021 saw unprecedented collapses; 2022+ recovery was rapid but uneven
6. **Low-Cost Carrier Dominance:** LCC capacity undercuts full-service carriers; market share is by passenger count, not revenue
7. **Charter Flights:** World Bank includes charter and non-scheduled flights; scheduled traffic is ~95%+ of total

## Refresh & Updates

Air travel indicators are updated annually via:

1. **World Bank API** — IS.AIR.PSGR, IS.AIR.DPRT, IS.AIR.GOOD.MT.K1 (1-2 year lag)
2. **Ministry of Civil Aviation** — Monthly aviation statistics reports (current-year updates)
3. **IATA & ICAO** — Industry forecasts and passenger/cargo benchmarks

The daily chart workflow (`scripts/generate_public_charts.py`) refreshes these indicators and crosschecks with:
- [Ministry of Civil Aviation Statistics Portal](https://www.civilaviation.gov.in/en/statistics-data)
- [IATA Air Transport Action Group (ATAG) reports](https://www.atag.org/)

## Why This Matters

India's aviation sector is a proxy for:

- **Economic Growth**: Air travel scales with per-capita income; rising passenger numbers signal growth
- **Urban Connectivity**: Aviation links metros and tier-1 cities; Regional Connectivity Scheme extends access
- **Export Orientation**: Air cargo growth (pharma, electronics) shows supply-chain integration
- **Global Integration**: International route expansion signals India's role in global commerce and tourism
- **Infrastructure Readiness**: Airport capacity constraints signal investment opportunities
- **Employment**: Airlines, airports, ground handling, and aviation services create high-skill jobs
- **Policy Effectiveness**: Open Skies liberalisation and RCS subsidies show how regulation shapes market dynamics

Tracking air travel unveils India's integration into global markets and the spread of economic opportunity beyond metros.
